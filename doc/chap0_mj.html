Iterators

Iterators of orders of simple groups and more

1.0.0

27 October 2026

Stefan Kohl
Email: sk239@st-andrews.ac.uk
Homepage: https://stefan-kohl.github.io/

Abstract

Iterators is a package for GAP 4 which implements iterators running over the orders and descriptions of non-abelian finite simple groups sorted by increasing order, and iterators running over the groups contained in various group libraries coming with GAP.
